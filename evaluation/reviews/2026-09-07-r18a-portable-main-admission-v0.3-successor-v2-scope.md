## Hypothesis

The report/source cycle can be removed without changing a scientific equation,
frontend contract, evidence score, timeout, source/CI comparison or R12 exclusion.
Source admission is independent of four explicitly derived reports. Report
consistency is a separate, fail-closed check. Neither check grants release or
scientific approval. A generation statement is byte-consistency evidence, not
authenticated execution: actual command exit, frozen logs, complete gates and
protected-main CI remain necessary.
