# Safety and use boundary

Tailing Future R1 is a low-consequence, reduced-unit thermochemical verification lab. Its heat field is an independent numerical carrier and A/B are passive internal labels. It is not a real material, chemical-reaction model, certification tool, process package, HAZOP/LOPA system or operating procedure.

Hard product boundaries:

- no autonomous output to PLC, DCS, SIS or equipment;
- no optimization of explosives, uncontrolled high-exotherm operation, toxic release, weapons materials or safety-interlock bypass;
- no fabricated claim that a coarse particle, latent vector or interpolated field is a real atom, reaction path or measured plant state;
- any future out-of-domain, conservation, sensor-conflict or uncertainty failure returns `abstain`;
- inverse design requires an independent forward solver and a separate experiment/safety review;
- engineering candidates remain advisory and require qualified human review.

Future industrial work must define its quantity of interest, applicability domain, validation hierarchy, prediction interval, applicable codes and enterprise change-control path before recommendations are enabled.
